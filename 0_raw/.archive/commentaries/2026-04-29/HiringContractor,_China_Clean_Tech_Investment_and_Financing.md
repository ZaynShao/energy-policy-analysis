# Hiring|Contractor, China Clean Tech Investment and Financing

**01**

**About RMI**

RMI is an independent nonprofit founded in 1982 that transforms global energy systems through market-driven solutions to align with a 1.5°C future and secure a clean, prosperous, zero-carbon future for all. We work in the world’s most critical geographies and engage businesses, policymakers, communities, and NGOs to identify and scale energy system interventions that will cut greenhouse gas emissions by at least 50 percent by 2030. RMI has offices in Basalt and Boulder, Colorado; New York City; Oakland, California; Washington, D.C.; Abuja, Nigeria; and Beijing, People’s Republic of China. For more information, please visit **www.rmi.org**.

  

In 2013, RMI began working in China as a part of a joint research project including the Energy Research Institute of China’s National Development and Reform Commission, Lawrence Berkeley National Labs’ China Energy Group, and Energy Foundation China. The group designed a road map, called Reinventing Fire: China, to promote renewables and reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions in the Chinese economy without compromising economic growth. This roadmap has become the basis for ongoing programmatic activity.

  

RMI continues to expand its Chinese presence, with our Beijing office serving as the hub for our nationwide engagements. In 2017, RMI was awarded an overseas NGO certificate by China, and the National Energy Administration is our supervising government agency in China. The Beijing office works on multiple parts of China’s energy transformation including heavy industry decarbonization, power sector reform, low-carbon mobility and freight solutions, low-carbon cities, transforming the building stock, advancing clean production of hydrogen, and establishing the foundation for a circular economy.

**02**

**The Role**

**RMI is seeking a short-term contractor to join our Clean Tech Investment and Financing team.** Our work focuses on advancing clean technology development, by leveraging innovative financing solutions, to support China’s decarbonization agenda. The contractor will collaborate closely with a dynamic, multidisciplinary team of experts, working together to drive innovative financing solutions for clean technologies. The role requires strong teamwork, communication, and the ability to integrate insights across various project streams. The contractor will primarily support at least one of the following areas:

  

- 

Conduct in-depth market research on the financing landscape for clean technologies, including renewable energy, energy efficiency, carbon capture, and storage technologies.

  

- 

Analyze investment trends, barriers, and opportunities for clean tech startups and established companies.

  

- 

Identify and assess innovative financial instruments and mechanisms that can support the deployment of clean technologies.

  

- 

Prepare detailed reports and presentations on research findings, including data visualization, to support policy recommendations and business strategies.

  

- 

Collaborate with internal teams to integrate research insights into broader project objectives and stakeholder engagement.

  

- 

Monitor global and regional regulatory developments affecting clean tech financing.

**03**

**Candidate Requirements**

**A successful candidate is desired to have: **

  

- 

Advanced degree in environmental science, economics, finance, or a related field is preferred.

  

- 

Proven experience in clean tech market research, particularly in financing and investment. And a strong understanding of related policy environments.

  

- 

Demonstrated expertise in financial analysis, including evaluating funding mechanisms (venture capital, private equity, green bonds, etc.).

  

- 

Excellent research, analytical, and bilingual writing skills.

  

- 

Ability to translate complex research findings into clear and actionable insights for diverse stakeholders.

**04**

**Compensation and Application**

A very competitive compensation will be offered to the final candidate selected. **If you are interested, please send your resume in both Chinese and English to llu@rmi.org.** Applications will be reviewed and processed on a rolling basis, so you are encouraged to submit them at your earliest convenience.   

**05**

**Location**

Beijing, China
