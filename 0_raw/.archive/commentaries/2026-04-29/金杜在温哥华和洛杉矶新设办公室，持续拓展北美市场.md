# 金杜在温哥华和洛杉矶新设办公室，持续拓展北美市场

![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/sz_mmbiz_png/WWbwrx83bPCSYSnOI0XniaEpGpIE8ce4JtnJYIIBqVd53BjkB2Lia2ycppBKBVNNHMxsfxHQVRXrfUwglwJgwPEyUeGoLibBwPaPDuzhlicIfTQ/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

近日，金杜律师事务所在加拿大温哥华和美国洛杉矶设立新办公室，持续提升在北美地区的服务能力，并迈出国际化发展战略的重要一步。温哥华与洛杉矶地处环太平洋经济圈的核心地带，是连接中国、北美以及环太平洋更广泛地区之间国际贸易、投资与资本流动的重要引擎。

此次战略布局反映了当前中加经贸往来持续深化，中国海外投资贸易日益活跃，以及客户对跨境贸易、投资与财富管理等领域法律服务需求显著增长的市场背景。温哥华与洛杉矶是中国企业及高净值人士登陆北美市场的重要门户，也是北美企业进入亚洲市场的关键出海港口。我们在两地的新办公室将成为金杜全球服务网络的进一步延展。

**温哥华：把握中加投资的亚太门户**

温哥华作为加拿大面向亚太的重要门户，与中国乃至更广泛的亚太地区拥有深厚的商业、文化与投资连接。随着中加经贸关系的迅速回暖，金杜注意到客户在中加之间的企业设立、并购、投资、合规、国际贸易、税务规划、争议解决及财富管理等方面的法律服务需求呈现增长态势。

新设立的温哥华办公室注册为King & Wood LLP，是加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚省法律下的有限责任合伙，将由胡再持律师与刘成律师共同牵头。胡再持律师具有在加拿大、中国和国际领先的律师事务所超过二十年的跨文化、多法域执业经验，长期为跨国客户就外商直接投资、并购、融资及公司与商业事务提供专业服务。刘成律师为金杜公司业务部合伙人，有中国及纽约执业许可，在反垄断、跨境监管及并购等领域经验丰富。温哥华办公室将致力于提供协同高效的中加法律服务，全力支持中加企业及投资者共同发展，拓展两国市场。

**洛杉矶：强化美国西海岸业务布局**

随着洛杉矶办公室的设立，金杜将进一步强化在美国西海岸的业务布局及专业能力，与现有的纽约办公室和硅谷办公室形成有力协同。洛杉矶办公室将为中美客户在跨境投资、银行与融资、贸易、科技、媒体及财富管理等领域提供专业支持。吕膺昊律师将牵头洛杉矶办公室的工作，他将同时担任金杜美国银行与融资业务负责人。吕律师为国际及中资商业银行、投资银行、投资基金与实业企业提供各类融资交易法律服务，尤其专注于国际项目融资、杠杆收购融资、贸易融资及资产融资业务。他在矿业和能源行业的公司型合资或非公司型合资交易中代表投资人。

**King & Wood New Offices Established in Vancouver and Los Angeles, Expanding its North American Footprint**

King & Wood today announces the establishment of affiliated offices in Vancouver and Los Angeles,  strengthening its North American platform and marking a significant next phase in the firm's international growth strategy. Both cities sit at the heart of the Pacific Rim economy, which is a primary engine of global trade, investment and capital flows linking China, North America and the wider Pacific Rim region.

The expansion reflects increasing client demand driven by renewed China–Canada economic engagement, growing outbound Chinese investment and trade, and the rising need for cross-border legal services across trade, investment and private wealth matters. Both Vancouver and Los Angeles are important gateways for Chinese enterprises and high-net-worth individuals operating in North America, as well as key seaports for North American companies entering the Asian market, and our affiliated offices in these two locations will serve as further extensions of King & Wood's international network.

**Vancouver: Asia-Pacific Gateway for China–Canada Investment**

Vancouver occupies a unique position as Canada's Asia-Pacific gateway, with deep commercial, cultural and investment ties to China and the wider region. As China–Canada economic and trade relations rapidly recover, King & Wood is seeing sustained growth in client demand across China and Canada in corporate establishment, M&A, investment, compliance, international trade, tax planning, disputes and private wealth matters.

The new Vancouver affiliated office is officially registered as King & Wood LLP (a limited liability partnership under the laws of British Columbia) and will be co-led by Hu Zaichi and Liu Cheng. Mr. Hu brings more than two decades of cross-cultural and multijurisdictional experience from leading Canadian, Chinese and international law firms, serving multinational clients in foreign direct investment, mergers and acquisitions, financing, and corporate and commercial matters. Mr. Liu is a corporate partner of King & Wood with extensive experience in antitrust, cross-border regulatory compliance and M&A (admitted to practice in China and New York). Together, the Vancouver office will focus on delivering coordinated China–Canada legal services and working with local partners to support Chinese and Canadian enterprises and investors as they collaborate and expand into both markets.

**Los Angeles: Strengthening the US West Coast Platform**

With the opening of its Los Angeles office, King & Wood has further strengthened its presence on the US West Coast and added additional presence and capabilities to its existing New York and Silicon Valley offices. The Los Angeles office will support Chinese and American clients across cross-border investment, banking and finance, trade, technology, media and private wealth matters.  Lu Yinghao will head up the Los Angeles office and will also become the head of the firm's Banking & Finance practice in the US. Yinghao represents international and Chinese commercial banks, investment banks, investment funds and industrial businesses in a wide variety of financing transactions, with a particular focus on international project financing, leveraged acquisition finance, trade finance and asset finance. He also advises miners and energy investors in cross-border incorporated and unincorporated joint venture transactions.

The firm's Global Chairman, **Wang Junfeng** commented:

 "*North America is a critical market for our clients, and this expansion reflects the growing scale and sophistication of Chinese investment into Canada and the United States. Vancouver and Los Angeles are natural hubs for cross-border work, and the new overseas offices mark a key step in King & Wood's international strategy, embodying our firm commitment to supporting clients’ global business development.*"

The firm's Managing Partner, **Gong Mulong** added:

*"The establishment of these new offices demonstrates our resolve to steadily advance our international strategy. By integrating trusted local resources and seamlessly aligning them with King & Wood's global network, we are committed to continuously building and enhancing our ability to serve clients across North America and safeguarding their interests amid an increasingly complex regulatory landscape."*

**Meg Utterback**, the firm's North America Managing Partner said:

*"The Vancouver and Los Angeles offices will significantly enhance our ability to serve our clients across North America. By deepening our West Coast presence, we are strengthening cross-border connectivity and delivering more integrated, responsive legal support to clients operating across China, Canada and the United States."*
