# Hiring|Project-based Contractor, Heavy Industry and Recycling Initiative

About RMI
Rocky Mountain Institute (RMl) is an independent, nonpartisan nonprofit founded in 1982 that transforms global energy systems through market-driven solutions to secure a prosperous, resilient, clean energy future for all. In collaboration with businesses, policymakers, funders, communities, and other partners, RMl drives investment to scale clean energy solutions, reduce energy waste, and boost access to affordable clean energy in ways that enhance security, strengthen the economy, and improve people's livelihoods. RMl is active in over 50 countries.
The China team collaborates with stakeholders, key experts, businesses, academia, and industry leaders to provide in-depth technical expertise and conduct impact-driven research. We leverage systematic approach and market-focused mechanisms to accelerate decarbonization in power, heavy industries, building, transportation, methane, and other key sectors.
Background
RMI China’s Heavy Industry and Recycling Initiative works to advance the transition of China’s key industrial sectors-steel, cement, chemicals, and aluminum-through supply, demand, and ecosystem levers: scaling renewables, green hydrogen, and circularity on the supply side; enabling green premiums, low-carbon standards, demand aggregation, and innovative mechanisms on the demand side; and fostering collaborative commitment, focused forums, and in-depth engagement across the ecosystem. Our approach is market-driven and value-chain integrated—building a sustainable, prosperous industrial system.
The Role
RMI is seeking an exceptional candidate to join our China Program’s Heavy Industry and Recycling Initiative. This project-based contractor position will support research and analysis on industrial decarbonization, with a particular focus on the chemicals sector, including both coal chemical and petrochemical industries. The role will involve assessing decarbonization pathways, emerging technologies, industry trends, and enabling mechanisms, while contributing to the development of practical solutions that support the sector's transition toward a low-carbon future.
Scope of Works
Analyze progress across industrial sectors. Evaluate cross-sector synergies and enabling factors that support industrial symbiosis and low-carbon transition.
Research and evaluate decarbonization pathways for the chemicals sector, including clean energy integration, feedstock transition, process innovation, carbon capture and utilization, circular carbon approaches, and low-carbon product development.
Conduct techno-economic assessments of key decarbonization technologies, projects, and business models to identify cost-effective pathways for industrial decarbonization.
Support the development of research reports, stakeholder engagement activities, and other project deliverables
Other relevant work as assigned.
Candidate Requirements
Master’s degree or above, preferably with relevant experience in environment and energy field.
Professional-level Chinese language skills, including strong Chinese reading and writing capabilities for research and stakeholder engagement.
Excellent written and spoken English skills, ability to use English as a working language.
Strong learning and analytical abilities, intellectual curiosity, and attention to detail.
A collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced team environment.
Contract Term & Compensation
Availability of 4–5 working days per week, with a preference for candidates able to work on-site at least 3 days each week.
Flexible contract duration, with a minimum engagement period of 6 months.
Competitive compensation commensurate with qualifications and relevant experience.
