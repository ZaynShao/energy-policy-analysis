# 重磅发布：《能源与矿产——全球布局：能源矿产资源之国别指南与新兴市场合规要点》

![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/WWbwrx83bPCrWHCj1cCJJia9QCficU7COsh00JiclmNGFeMRicdm4udWeM6fticSJSibknTbOoOq2fI2X34fTv5mmxTEic6Jv1NsCK2AMUPUNzTxyc/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

能源，是人类文明演进的底层支撑，也是大国博弈与全球治理的核心变量。当前，世界正处于深刻的能源体系重构之中。在此背景下，中国“十五五”规划对构建现代能源体系的战略部署，既是对全球能源转型大势的积极回应，也是引领变革的主动作为。同时，中国的能源产业，正以更深的层次融入全球分工，参与技术协作与规则塑造。

金杜律师事务所在能源与矿产领域的国际化法律团队，长期深耕实务前沿，熟悉中国本土政策环境与监管实践，同时具备扎实的全球视野和跨境交易执行能力。从传统油气资产到风光氢储新能源项目，从关键矿产跨境布局到数据中心与AI算力基础设施的能源合规，我们的工作始终与产业发展同频共振。

凭借对行业趋势的深刻理解、对商业逻辑的准确把握，以及在全球主要能源市场积累的丰富实务经验，金杜能源与矿产法律团队撰写了《能源与矿产——全球布局：能源矿产资源之国别指南与新兴市场合规要点》，本书聚焦能源转型所需的矿产资源，解析投资资源领域的核心法律门槛，并提供新兴市场投资参考。我们期待与客户携手，在能源变革的大潮中把握方向，共筑可持续发展的未来。

Against this backdrop, the strategic deployment set forth in China’s 15th Five-Year Plan for building a modern energy system represents not only an active response to the global energy transition, but also a proactive effort to lead the transformation. Meanwhile, China's energy industry is integrating into the global division of labor at a deeper level, engaging in technological collaboration and the shaping of international rules.

King & Wood's international legal team in the energy and minerals sector has long been active at the forefront of practice. The team is well-versed in China’s domestic policy environment and regulatory practices, while possessing a strong global perspective and proven capabilities in cross-border transactions. From traditional oil and gas assets to renewable energy projects in wind, solar, hydrogen, and energy storage, and from cross-border deployment of critical minerals to energy compliance for data centers and AI computing infrastructure, our work remains closely aligned with the industry developments.

Drawing on deep insights into industry trends, a precise understanding of commercial logic, and extensive hands-on experience across major energy markets worldwide, King & Wood’s energy and minerals team has authored *Energy & Minerals** – Global Footprint: A Country Guide to Energy and Mineral Resources and Key Compliance Considerations in Emerging Markets*. This publication focuses on mineral resources essential to the energy transition, analyzes the core legal requirements for investing in the resources sector, and provides a reference for investment in emerging markets. We look forward to partnering with our clients to navigate the energy transformation and jointly build a sustainable future.
