As China pursues higher-standard opening-up and optimizes the foreign investment environment, multinational businesses face a dual challenge: capturing new market opportunities while navigating an increasingly complex compliance landscape. To help navigate this environment, we are launching the "China in a Changing World" series. Featuring insights from cross-practice lawyers at King & Wood, this series breaks down the latest trends and legal developments across various industries, offering practical insights for overseas investors.
