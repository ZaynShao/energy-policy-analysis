# China in a Changing World Series | Guide to Digital Assets in Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a leading global digital assets hub with a dynamic Web3 ecosystem and a robust digital asset regulatory framework balancing investor protection, market integrity and sustainable innovation.
Andrew Fei, King & Wood Hong Kong office partner, unpacks Hong Kong's digital asset regulatory framework, clarifying current licensing requirements for tokenised financial products and virtual assets, and what's coming next.

Areas of Practice: banking and finance
Andrew Fei has over 15 years of experience in the areas of digital assets, tokenisation, digital payments, financial regulation, structured finance, derivatives, syndicated loans, capital markets and securitisation. Andrew’s clients include central banks, all three note-issuing banks in Hong Kong, global financial institutions, industry associations, listed companies and fintech giants. Andrew has published over 100 articles on digital assets and financial regulation and is regularly quoted as an expert in the international press, including the Financial Times, New York Times, Bloomberg Businessweek, and South China Morning Post. Andrew holds a master of laws degree from Harvard Law School, and master and bachelor of laws degrees (double first class honors) from Cambridge University, where he ranked second in his class. He is qualified to practice law in New York and Hong Kong. In addition to his full-time legal practice, Andrew is an Adjunct Associate Professor at the University of Hong Kong where he teaches banking law at the Faculty of Law.
